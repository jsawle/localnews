Finding which communities have a newsletter

No register of UK local newsletters exists. This is the order of work that builds one.

  1. Get the PINF base layer. Email PINF for the CSV behind the Local News Map. It covers every local authority district and flags corporate, independent and IPSO-regulated outlets.
  2. Add the networks. Mill Media (7 cities), Nub News (about 25 towns) and the ICNN member map (130+ independents) are listable from their own sites.
  3. Search the platforms town by town. Take ONS built-up area names and search Substack, Ghost and Beehiiv for each name plus "news". One-person titles such as Don't Miss Margate only turn up this way.
  4. Check each hit for original reporting. Separate reporting from listings and aggregated digests. Bylines, staff count and council coverage are workable tests.
  5. Geoparse recent issues. Extract the place names each newsletter actually writes about. That gives real coverage, not claimed coverage.
  6. Map at built-up area level and overlay. Compare newsletter coverage with PINF deserts and LDRS contract areas. Test whether newsletters fill deserts or cluster in places already served.

Known limits

Counting outlets is a proxy. It says nothing about quality, reach or whether council meetings get covered.

PINF's first map (2023) was disputed by Newsquest and Reach. Regional titles covering several councils made some areas look like deserts, and unincorporated hyperlocals were missed.

Newsletters open and close fast. Nub News closed 17 towns in 18 months, so any map is a snapshot.

Subscriber numbers are mostly private. Presence does not tell you reach.

What to test

PINF's 2025 report found new launches, mostly independent and digital, are more common in affluent districts. Closures hit the most deprived hardest. The overlay in step 6 is the way to check that for newsletters specifically.

Add your own data

Load a CSV such as the PINF outlet file or your own newsletter search results. Columns are matched by name: name, lat/latitude, lon/lng/longitude, and optionally place, type, url, notes. Rows without coordinates are placed if the place name matches one already on the map.

About this atlas

A working tool for assessing local news provision across the UK. It combines the published figures from the Public Interest News Foundation, BBC Local News Partnerships, DCMS and the trade press with a hand-built layer of newsletter networks.

Points mark a town or city centre, not a coverage area. News desert markers sit at the district centre. Coordinates are approximate.

Research compiled 25 September 2026. Figures change often; check the linked sources before quoting them.

Sources: PINF Local News Map and 2023–2025 reports; DCMS Local Media Action Plan (Mar 2026) and Local News Fund (Jul 2026); BBC LDRS 2025 contract notice; Press Gazette; HoldtheFrontPage; ICNN; Mill Media; Ofcom Review of Local Media (Jul 2024). Links in Data sources.
